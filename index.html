Questions people actually ask

Straight answers

What does a digital marketing specialist actually do for a small business?

Rather than running one-off tasks, my job is to connect the whole chain — attention, interest, lead capture, nurture, offer and sale — so your marketing spend turns into booked work instead of stopping at likes and reach. Most businesses don't need more marketing. They need the parts they already have to talk to each other.

I'm just starting my business. Do I need a funnel or just a website?

Almost always: one clear page with one clear action, before anything else. Capture and follow-up first, then paid traffic, then scale. Building it in the opposite order is the single most common way I see new businesses burn their first budget.

Why do my Meta ads get clicks but no customers?

Nearly always the break is after the click — a slow or confusing landing page, no real lead capture, or no automated follow-up. Reporting that stops at CTR and CPC hides this completely. Track it through to cost per booked call and the leak shows up immediately.

Which platforms do you build in?

Mainly GoHighLevel and Kajabi for funnels, CRM and automation; Meta Business Suite and Ads Manager for paid campaigns; Zapier for connecting everything else. I'll also work inside whatever you're already paying for rather than forcing a migration you don't need.

Do you only work with businesses in the Philippines?

No — I'm based in General Santos City and work with clients worldwide, remotely. I've worked across health and allied-health practices, coaching brands, wellness, education and community organisations. I communicate in English, Filipino and Cebuano, and adapt to British or US spelling depending on your audience.

What if I don't know what's wrong yet?

That's the normal starting point, and honestly the most useful one. You describe what's happening, I'll tell you which link in the chain is breaking and what I'd fix first — whether or not you end up working with me.
