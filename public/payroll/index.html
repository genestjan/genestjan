How times are read
  • A period works like a colon: 7.15 is 7:15, and 12.8 is 12:08.
  • 3 or 4 digits with no colon become H:MM: 130 is 1:30, 1207 is 12:07, 1500 is 15:00. 1 or 2 digits are whole hours: 8 is 8:00.
  • If an end time is earlier than its start, 12 hours are added, so 12:10 to 3 is 2:50. For long blocks use 24-hour time: 6 to 18, not 6 to 6. You can also type am and pm, like 8a or 5:30p.
  • Decimal hours are hours plus minutes divided by 60, rounded to 2 places: 94:36 is 94.60.
