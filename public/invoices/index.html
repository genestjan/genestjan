Sync laptop and phone

Off

Keeps every device on the same invoices, clients and settings. The data is saved in a secret gist on your GitHub account, locked with your password, so GitHub only ever stores scrambled text. You can use the same token as the Payroll app.

  1. Make a GitHub token. Set Expiration to No expiration, keep only gist ticked, click Generate token and copy it.
  2. Paste it here and turn sync on.

Is sync already on for your other device? Use its setup link instead (below), and this device joins automatically.

Team

Off

Your partners get paid in Payroll and log their time in this same app on their own phone or computer, with their own password. There they only see the clients and projects you give them and their own time: never your invoices, rates, payroll or each other's time. Client reports and invoices never show who did the work.

Partner logins

  1. Make a separate, free GitHub account just for the team (for example yourname-team). Not your own: partners get its token.
  2. Signed in as that account, make a token with No expiration and only gist ticked.
  3. Paste it here. Then Invite link on each partner gives them their login.
