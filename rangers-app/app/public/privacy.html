Privacy notice

The Rangers App is run by volunteers of Rangers Football Club, with FC New Mabu and Gila Academy, in General Santos City, Philippines. It is used to organise training and matches and to keep coaches, parents and players in touch. It is free and has no ads.

What we keep

Your name and email address; your child's name, birth date, shirt number and team; attendance, playing time and football progress; your child's address, phone number and the health details you choose to give (emergency contacts, medication, allergies, inhaler, injuries at the club, height for growth spurts); photos your child sends as proof for tasks from the coach. Coaches may share how-to videos through unlisted YouTube links; children do not upload videos. A photo of your child's student ID and PSA birth certificate, because tournament and school-meet organisers ask the coach to show them. For school teams, which DepEd screening papers are ready (PSA birth certificate, Form 137 / SF10, dental record, parental consent, medical clearance), not the papers themselves. A photo of your child is shown on team screens only if you say yes.

Feedback about the app

If you send feedback, the club admins and the people they choose to read it see your name, whether you are a parent or coach, and your message. Feedback is kept for two years. Please do not write your child's health details in it.

Why

To run the club safely: contacting families, looking after children in an emergency, planning fair playing time and following progress. We do not use it for anything else.

Who sees it

Your child's coaches and the club admins. You see only your own child. Other parents never see your child's reviews, health information or contributions. Screens shown to children use first names and shirt numbers only.

Sign-in

You can sign in with your Google account. We receive only your name and email address from Google. We do not read your contacts, emails or files.

Where it is stored

On the club's account at Supabase (a database service), and on the phones of the coaches and families who use the app. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone outside the club, except the service providers that store it for us.

How long

While your child is with the club, then it is deleted 12 months after they leave. Photos of ID papers are deleted as soon as your child leaves the club.

Your rights

You can change photo and video permission at any time, download your child's information, correct it, or ask us to delete it, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173). Contact Coach James: +63 993 291 0764.

Basahin sa Filipino

Ang mga coach at admin lang ng club ang makakakita ng impormasyon ng inyong anak, at kayo lang ang makakakita ng sa inyong anak. Buburahin ito 12 buwan pagkatapos umalis ang bata. Puwede ninyong baguhin ang pahintulot sa litrato, i-download, itama o ipabura ang impormasyon anumang oras.
