GenDrive

The real layout of v0.1 at the eMAX 7's 12.8" screen size. Tap anything on the screen.

Preview made from the app's own layout and colours. Car numbers here come from the built-in demo drive. Song and lyrics are made up for the demo.

Why it looks like this

Controls on your side

The rail sits on the driver's side and the assistant button is at the bottom of it, where your hand already rests. Right-hand-drive cars get a mirrored layout.

Readable in a two-second glance

One big number per card. Nothing you need to read is smaller than 16 points, and every target is at least 72 points tall.

It calms down when you drive

Moving hides the savings card, cuts lyrics to the current and next line, locks typing and settings, and keeps video apps to sound only until you shift to P.

Money you can feel

Every trip ends with a "Trip complete" card and a spoken summary of what it cost and how much you saved against petrol, in pesos.

Colours that mean something

Mint means good or go. Blue is navigation. Amber and coral appear only when something needs attention. Violet is the assistant.

Gen shows what it's doing

Tap the purple button and the line under the greeting says Listening, then what Gen heard, then its reply. You never wonder whether it heard you.

Honest data

The pill at the top says whether numbers are live from the car or from the demo drive. Anything the car doesn't report shows a dash, never a guess.
